2 - Potential Customer Estimation: Spatial Method Selection

Testing three service models to assess theoretically covered population:

  • Method 1: Euclidean buffer (straight line) around the line route.
  • Method 2: Euclidean buffer around each bus stop.
  • Method 3: 5-minute real walking isochrone zone on the pedestrian network.

Result: Method 3 (isochrones) eliminates topographical biases and is retained as the reference.
